How to film

Drop in a walk-through video.
Get a tour anyone can walk.

One upload in, one shareable link out. Works with MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV, AVI, M4V, MPEG and 3GP — phone, gimbal or 360° camera.

For the smoothest tour

  • 🚶 Walk slowly, camera at chest height
  • 🔁 Cross back through doorways where you want a branch
  • 🔄 Pause and slowly spin in the middle of each room
  • 💡 Lights on, no people walking in frame
  • 🌐 360° camera? You'll get free look-around
