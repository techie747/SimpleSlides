How to film a great tour

Two minutes of care while filming saves every visitor from a confusing tour.

  1. Walk slowly and steadily, camera at chest height, pointing where you walk.
  2. Lights on. Dark or blurry footage can't be mapped.
  3. Overlap your route at doorways and hallways — walking through a spot twice creates a branch visitors can choose.
  4. Spin slowly once in the middle of each room. That spin becomes the Turn Left / Turn Right view.
  5. Keep people out of frame and avoid pointing at blank walls for long.
  6. Have a 360° camera? Use it — visitors get full free look-around at every step.

Any of these files work: MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV, AVI, M4V, MPEG/MPG, 3GP, up to 4 GB / 20 minutes.
